# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack
Standalone single-file HTML previews (the owner/freelancer opens them by double-click, no server). Source is React 18 + framer-motion, bundled and inlined with photos and fonts by `clients/jbs-pro-painters/v3/build.mjs`. No Netlify required to preview; Netlify Forms is the intended form handler once hosted (mailto fallback in previews).

## Users
- Homeowners and small business owners (restaurants, warehouses, shops) in the East Valley of Arizona (Gilbert, Mesa, Chandler, Tempe, Scottsdale, Queen Creek) who need interior or exterior painting and want to see real finished work before they call. Mostly on phones. They decide by looking at before/after proof, then call, text or send the free-estimate form.
- The freelancer building these samples (the user) pitches them to the owner, Jonathan Bernal, who picks one direction.

## Product Purpose
Win free-estimate requests for JB'S Pro Painters LLC, a licensed and insured, owner-operated painting company. Success = a visitor sees real proof, trusts the work, and calls, texts or submits the estimate form.

## Positioning
Visible before/after proof from real jobs (homes, a restaurant exterior, a warehouse), shown at the same spot. Residential and commercial treated equally. Tagline: Quality, Precision, Perfection.

## Operating Context
Owner Jonathan Bernal, (602) 473-0415, jbspropainters@gmail.com. Service area Gilbert, Mesa, Chandler, Tempe, Scottsdale, Queen Creek and more. Facebook "JB'S Pro Painters LLC", Instagram @jbspropainters. Free estimates by form, call or text.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Services supported by photos: exterior painting, interior painting, cabinet refinishing, doors and wood stain, commercial repaints (restaurant exterior, warehouse), accent walls.
- Not confirmed, do not claim: license number, years in business, insurance amounts, warranty, pricing, paint brands, number of reviews beyond one Facebook review.
- Third-party brands visible in some photos (Thirsty Lion, KTM/Ducati, AAA Sun Control, Tuff Shed): do not show or name without the owner's approval. The restaurant is shown as "restaurant exterior".
- Most new photos are about 480px wide; hero-scale use needs the owner's originals.

## Product Principles
1. Proof before claims: every strong statement is backed by a real photo pair.
2. One clear action: a free estimate, always one tap away.
3. Say only what is confirmed; mark placeholders honestly.
4. Mobile first: most visitors are on a phone with one thumb.
5. Residential and commercial get equal weight.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, keyboard operable controls, visible focus, reduced-motion support, sound is opt-in and off by default.
