# Awesome DESIGN.md

Source: https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md (MIT).

Each brand lives at `design-md/<brand>/DESIGN.md` (tokens, typography, color, components, do/don't). List brands with `ls design-md`.

1. Pick the closest brand(s) for the brief; read their `DESIGN.md`.
2. Borrow structure and rules (spacing, type scale, component behavior), not the brand's name, logo or copy.
3. For FPTSPORTZ and JB'S work, the client's own brand colors still win.
